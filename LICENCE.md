## 🧩 About the Leak

In August 2026, an individual or group named Cyberleak leaked an early Alpha build of Grand Theft Auto VI. The leaker confirmed access to a playable build by spelling "LEEK" with bullet holes in-game.

**Key Details:**
- **Build Era:** 2022 – early 2023
- **Leak Start:** August 16, 2026
- **Content:** Over 2 hours of gameplay footage
- **Map:** Full Vice City region
- **Confirmed:** Playable build access
- **Protagonists:** Jason appears in all clips; Lucia not yet shown
